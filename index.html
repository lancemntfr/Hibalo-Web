Learn and translate Hiligaynon, even offline.

Hibalo is an Android app that translates between Hiligaynon and Filipino and teaches Hiligaynon through short lessons and quizzes.

See how it works

A capstone project of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines · Demo available on request

What it does

Translate, learn and practice in one app.

Look up a phrase, hear how it sounds, then practice it in a lesson. After you register once, translation keeps working without internet.

  • Translate

    Hiligaynon and Filipino, by text, voice or camera. Listen to the Hiligaynon result spoken aloud.

  • Learn

    Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced levels, plus an alphabet module and native-speaker audio.

  • Practice

    Many quiz types with XP and a daily streak to keep you coming back. Learn a little each day.

  • Pronunciation

    Record yourself, compare with a native speaker and get a Very close, Almost or Try again result.

Also included

  • Voice input
  • Listen to results
  • Camera scan
  • Saved words
  • History
  • App in English, Filipino or Hiligaynon

Why Hibalo

A language that is fading at home.

In Sta. Maria, Sto. Tomas City, Batangas, many Hiligaynon speakers moved from the Visayas for work and brought their language with them. Parents taught basic words at home, but outside the home Tagalog took over, and children grew up more used to Tagalog.

There was no community program and no dedicated digital tool for Hiligaynon, so keeping the language alive relied on families. Hibalo was built to give speakers and learners one place to translate, hear and practice it.

  • 7.3%

    of Philippine households speak Hiligaynon at home (2020 Census, PSA)

  • 3rd

    most widely spoken home language in the country

Built with native speakers and real language data

About 5,000 Hiligaynon–Filipino translation pairs were gathered in person from native speakers and verified by the team and a native Hiligaynon speaker. These were combined with around 60,000 sentence pairs from public dictionaries and Bible translations, and over 400,000 Hiligaynon and Filipino sentences from the web, so the translator works fully offline on the phone.

FAQ

Questions and answers.

Do I need internet?

Only to register. After that, translation, voice input and camera scan work without internet.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer.

Do I need to know Filipino?

Not necessarily. The app can be set to English, Filipino or Hiligaynon, and lessons are in English and Filipino. The translator works between Hiligaynon and Filipino.

How accurate are the translations?

They are built from native-speaker data and designed as a learning aid. For formal or important documents, check with a fluent speaker.
